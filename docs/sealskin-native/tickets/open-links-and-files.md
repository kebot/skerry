# Open links and files in remote apps

Accept pasted URLs and files chosen or dropped in the launcher. Offer apps that declare URL support or matching extensions. Use the correct SealSkin launch endpoint, with progress and a choice to open or stage a file. Evaluate macOS Open With or Share integration after the basic flow. Browser context menus, authenticated downloads, and Chrome download interception require browser integration.
