Design notes

Q36: Connection colour vs Tab Group colour

Decision. Connection identity, copied from Arc: a small coloured dot on the right edge of every Tab, with a distinct shape per Connection (● ◆ ▲ ■) as the non-colour cue. The initial letter appears where there is room: Connection chips, address bar, dialog. The Sidebar background is also tinted faintly with the Active Tab's Connection colour, as Arc tints by Space. Tab Group identity = an outlined pill header and indent line from a separate, reserved palette. Active Tab uses its Connection colour as a ring and marker bar. The favicon keeps the left slot.

Why the proposed stripe failed

  • The 3 px stripe sits next to the Group indent line: two coloured verticals compete, especially in a cross-Connection Group.
  • Colour is the only cue: fails for colour-blind users and in greyscale (see gallery).
  • A 3 px sliver is low contrast against the Sidebar, and the Active Tab needs the left edge for its own marker.

Why the badge works

  • Two redundant cues: colour and shape. Dots are small and filled, never outlined, so Groups (outline + line) never look alike.
  • Groups use outline + line only; Connections use fill only. Different visual grammar, so they never read as the same thing.
  • Group palette (violet, olive, stone) excludes blue, pink, amber, teal so a Group never looks like a Connection.
  • Chip letters use dark text on amber for contrast of at least 4.5:1.
  • Collapsed Group shows mini squares of the Connections inside, so the span is visible without expanding.

Rejected alternatives

  • Tint the whole row by Connection: clashes with Group colour and Active highlight, poor in dark mode.
  • Replace favicons with Connection glyph: loses the fastest page recogniser.
  • Group colour on the Tab's left stripe, Connection in favicon ring: favicons are too small for a reliable ring.

Q37: Connection status and New Tab

Decision. Keep the chip row at the bottom of the Sidebar, one compact badge-chip per Connection (not full-width names). A status dot on the corner differs by shape and colour: round green = connected, spinning amber ring = reconnecting, red square = Profile stopped, hollow grey ring = not connected. A violet ✦ at the top-left corner means an Agent is attached. Click = connect (or show popover when connected); hover opens a popover with state text and the right action (Connect, Retry now, Start, Disconnect). New Tab moves out of the chip into a dedicated “+ New Tab” row at the top of the Sidebar, which shows the target Connection badge; ⌘T uses the Active Tab's Connection. Chip hover still shows a small “+” for “new Tab in this Connection”, and a New Tab on a not-connected chip connects first.

Evaluation of the proposal

  • Kept: chips at the bottom (status is ambient, thumb-near, works in the icon rail as a column).
  • Changed: a permanent “+” per chip doubles the row width and adds visual noise; a hover “+” is kept as a power path, the primary path is the top row.
  • Changed: status by dot colour alone is weak; shape and motion added.
  • The top row answers “where will this go?” explicitly, which a hidden Cmd+T rule does not.

Rejected alternatives

  • Connection headers in the Tab list: contradicts the single unified list.
  • Status in the address bar only: invisible for non-Active Connections.
  • Full-width chip rows with names: eat 4 or more rows at 5 Connections; names go in the popover and tooltip.
  • Top-bar Connection picker for New Tab: adds a click and shifts focus from the Mirror.

Open questions for the product owner

  • Cmd+T when the Active Tab's Connection is disconnected or stopped: connect (and start the Profile) automatically, or fall back to the last connected Connection?
  • Should connecting a Connection from a click on a Placeholder Tab also restore it as the Active Tab?
  • Beyond about 8 Connections the chip row wraps: acceptable, or scroll / overflow menu?
  • Can the user choose Group colours, or are they assigned from the reserved palette (needed to avoid Connection hues)?
  • Agent marker: is “touched earlier” (static) vs “acting now” (pulsing) worth two states in v1?
  • Same-colour Connections: enforce unique colours and initials in config validation?
